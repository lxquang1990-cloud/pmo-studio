# Review Rubric — Legal AI Customer-Style

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Completeness | Misses major modules | Covers modules but shallow | Covers modules, roles, workflows, NFRs |
| Correctness | Misstates legal governance | Mostly correct with minor gaps | Correct human-review and authority model |
| Specificity | Generic AI/legal wording | Some implementable details | Clear fields, states, permissions, audit events |
| Testability | Few testable AC/UAT items | Basic happy path | Happy/error/permission paths are testable |
| Estimate defensibility | Mandays arbitrary | Plausible with gaps | Assumptions/exclusions and ranges are explainable |
| Customer readiness | Major rewrite needed | Moderate BA edit needed | Minor edit before sending |

Minimum customer-ready bar: average >= 4.0 and no dimension below 3.
